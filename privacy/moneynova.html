MoneyNova Privacy Policy

MoneyNova is a personal finance and expense tracking application. The financial entries you create are stored on your device and are not uploaded to any server operated by the developer.

This application may use Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob (where enabled). Data is used only to provide functionality, analytics and crash reporting. No personal information is sold.

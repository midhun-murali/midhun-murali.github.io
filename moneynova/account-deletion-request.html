Delete Your MoneyNova Account

If you wish to permanently delete your MoneyNova account, follow one of the options below.

Option 1 (Recommended)

  1. Open the MoneyNova app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete Account

Your account and associated financial data will be permanently deleted.

Option 2

If you no longer have access to the app, email us at novalorefilms@gmail.com.

Please include:

  • Registered email address
  • Registered phone number (if applicable)

We will process your request within 7 days.

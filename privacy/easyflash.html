Easy Flash Privacy Policy

Easy Flash is a simple flashlight application that uses your device's camera flash to provide illumination. The app does not require access to your photos, contacts or location.

This application may use Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob (where enabled). Data is used only to provide functionality, analytics and crash reporting. No personal information is sold.

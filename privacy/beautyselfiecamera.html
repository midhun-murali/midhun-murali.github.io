Beauty Selfie Camera Privacy Policy

Beauty Selfie Camera is a photo editing application offering beauty retouching, filters and effects for selfies. Photos you edit are processed on your device and are not uploaded to any server operated by the developer.

This application may use Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob (where enabled). Data is used only to provide functionality, analytics and crash reporting. No personal information is sold.
